Editor guide

Everything you change here is saved as a draft first. Nothing goes live until it is approved and published.

How a change goes live

  1. Open what you want to change, edit it, and click Save. This creates a draft.
  2. Open the Workflow tab. Move the draft to In review when you are happy with it.
  3. The site owner approves it and clicks Publish. The site updates in about a minute.

Events

  • Repeating meetings (weekly or monthly) belong in the Area 30 Google Calendar. They appear in Explore events on their own.
  • One-time or special events that deserve a spotlight (an assembly, a workshop, a dinner) go in Events here. They are shown first, marked as highlighted.
  • Pick the Date, then the Starts at and Ends at times. All times are Eastern, whatever time zone you are in. Tick All-day event if there is no set time.
  • Past events drop off the lists by themselves.

Urgent messages

Use Site settings → Site notice for something people must see right away, such as a cancelled assembly or a changed Zoom link. It shows in a bar at the top of every page. Set a last day so it clears itself, or empty the message to remove it.

Districts

Fill in When it meets, then the online and in-person sections that apply. The card on the Districts page (and its Spanish and French versions) is written from those fields, and so are the next meeting date, the Add to calendar button, the Copy buttons and the Open in Zoom link. Use Other lines only for something that does not fit, because a district written only that way gets none of those extras. If a meeting has an exception (for example it moves when it falls on a holiday), put that in the Note box; the card then leaves out the computed next-meeting date so it cannot be wrong.

Editing the main pages

Home, Contribute, Resources, Literature, What is AA?, Registrar, 12th Step Volunteer, Calendar and Services are under Site settings. You edit the English text. Spanish and French drafts are prepared automatically and added once the site owner approves them. Until a bilingual AA member has checked them, search engines do not see them.

Each box with a list (questions, steps, links) lets you add, remove and reorder items. At the bottom of every page is Extra sections: add a text block, cards, callout, FAQ and so on, and they appear below the page's own sections, above the footer.

Where you see [[delegate|Delegate]], [words](https://...) or {email} in a box, leave those as they are. They make a definition popup, a link, or fill in an address automatically.

The sign-up and payment forms are not editable here, on purpose.

Taking something off the website

Do not use the Delete button. Instead, open the page, event, committee or district, tick Remove from the website, save, and publish. It disappears from the site, and a follow-up change cleans up the files.

Links

  • A page on this site starts with a slash, for example /committees/.
  • Another website starts with https://.
  • In the main menu, @documents is the Area 30 documents folder.

Pictures

  • Keep pictures under 1 MB. Large photos slow the site down for everyone.
  • Always fill in the picture description. It is read aloud to people who use screen readers and helps search engines.

Search descriptions

Each page has a one or two sentence description (about 120 to 160 characters) that appears in Google results. Write it for a person who is deciding whether to click.

Spanish and French

Spanish and French pages are hidden from search engines until a bilingual AA member has reviewed them. If you add a committee or district, it shows in English on those pages until someone adds a translation.

Stuck, or something looks wrong after publishing? Tell the site owner before changing it again.
